## The narrative reps tell

1. **Situation.** Your growth team ships campaigns weekly, and every question about what shoppers did becomes a ticket to a data team that is already behind.
2. **Shift.** Storefront stacks now emit rich events, so the bottleneck has moved from collecting data to asking it questions in merchandising terms.
3. **Problem.** General-purpose analytics tools model apps, not stores. Carts, variants and promotions arrive as custom work, and the schema rots.
4. **The Acme way.** A pre-built e-commerce model, funnels that understand collections and promotions, and answers that show their query.
5. **Proof.** [verify-before-external-use] Time to first answered question in onboarding cohorts — [CONFIRM] the current figure from catalog row R-05 before quoting. [verify-before-external-use] Reduction in analytics tickets at reference customers — [CONFIRM] with the two named references in the CRM.
6. **Ask.** A two-week pilot on one storefront with the growth lead's three open questions as the acceptance test.
