> This page describes how the company **and its agents** sound.

## Voice

Plain, specific, numerate, and calm. We sound like a colleague who has looked at the data, not like a launch video.

## Do and do not

| Do | Do not |
| --- | --- |
| Lead with the shopper behaviour, then the feature | Lead with adjectives ("powerful", "seamless", "revolutionary") |
| Give the definition with every number | Quote a number without its source and date |
| Use merchandising words (collection, promotion, variant) | Use analytics jargon the champion would not say |
| Sentence case headings; short paragraphs | Exclamation marks; all-caps emphasis |

## How agents apply this page

- Draft in this voice; cite the canon page URL when canon shaped the output.
- Label anything drawn from a draft page as unconfirmed, in the sentence itself.
- Never invent a customer name, quote or statistic. Offer to log a gap instead.
