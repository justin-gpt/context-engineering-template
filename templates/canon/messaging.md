## Core value proposition

Know what your shoppers did and why, in the words your merchandising team already uses, without waiting on a data request.

## Three pillars

| Pillar | Claim | Feature behind it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready on day one | A storefront connects in an afternoon and answers real questions the first week | Pre-built e-commerce event model |
| Merchandising native | Cut any funnel by category, collection or promotion | Merchandising-aware funnels |
| Trustworthy by design | Every answer shows its definition and query | Query-attached answers |

## Value by persona

- **Head of Growth:** fewer data requests, faster experiment read-outs, one shared definition of conversion.
- **Merchandising lead:** see which collections and promotions move the funnel, in merchandising language.
- **Founder or CEO:** one number per question, with the query attached, so the board pack reconciles.

## Boilerplate

**25 words:** Acme Analytics is product analytics for e-commerce teams: a pre-built event model, merchandising-aware funnels, and answers that show their work.

**50 words:** Acme Analytics gives mid-market e-commerce teams product analytics that speaks their language. A pre-built event model gets a storefront answering real questions in the first week, merchandising-aware funnels cut by category and promotion without a data request, and every answer shows the definition and query behind it.

## Claims we do not make

- Rankings or superlatives ("the leading", "number one") without a cited third-party source.
- Customer counts, revenue figures or growth rates in copy unless pulled live through the catalog and dated.
- "AI-powered" as a pillar. We describe what the feature does.
